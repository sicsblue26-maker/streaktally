Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StreakTally ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and any display name you choose to provide.
  • Habit data. The habits you create, the days you mark them complete, streaks, and any notes you add.
  • Health data (optional, Premium). If you connect Apple Health or Google Health Connect, we read selected metrics such as steps, sleep, workouts, or mindful minutes to automatically log related habits. Health data is only read after you explicitly grant permission for each metric, and it stays on your device unless you enable cloud sync.
  • Device and usage information. Basic technical data such as device type, operating system version, and app version, used for diagnostics and to keep the App working correctly.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's core features.
  • To sync your habits across your devices (Premium).
  • To send reminders and notifications you have enabled.
  • To display your progress, streaks, and statistics.
  • To respond to support requests and account deletion requests.
  • To detect, prevent, and fix technical issues.

3. Health Data

When you link Apple Health or Health Connect, StreakTally reads only the data types you explicitly approve. We do not use health data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

Health data is processed on your device. If you enable cloud sync, it is transmitted to our backend provider over an encrypted connection. You can revoke access at any time in your device's Health settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Service providers. We use Supabase for authentication and data storage. Supabase processes data on our behalf under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • App stores. Apple and Google may collect data as part of app distribution and in-app purchases.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your account and associated habit data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a portable copy of your data.

To exercise these rights, email us at dzubvurand@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

StreakTally is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
